Rules & safety

Keep Forks Land clean, respectful, and secure.

1

Respect everyone

Treat members and staff respectfully. Harassment and targeted abuse are not welcome.

2

Follow Discord rules

Follow Discord's Terms, Community Guidelines, and applicable laws while using the server.

3

No spam or disruption

Avoid spam, malicious links, disruptive behavior, and attempts to interfere with the community.

4

Respect staff decisions

Use the appropriate support channels if you believe moderation action needs review.

🍯

Honeypot alert

Think you found a secret channel? Forks Land has an active Honeypot System designed to catch unauthorized access, suspicious activity, and rule-breakers. Restricted channels may be monitored and activity may be logged for Staff review. Attempting to bypass permissions or access restricted areas may result in moderation action.
